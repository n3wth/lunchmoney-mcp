# Client icons

Official website icons, retrieved September 15, 2026. These marks belong to their respective owners and identify compatible or planned clients; they are not covered by this project's MIT license.

- ChatGPT: OpenAI mark from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/openai-agents-python/main/docs/assets/logo.svg, displayed in black with a transparent background and a tight viewBox.
- Codex: standalone symbol from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/glincker/thesvg@main/public/icons/codex/default.svg, displayed in blue without an app tile at the user's request. This is a third-party vector reproduction, not an official downloadable brand asset.
- Claude (Claude, Claude Code and Cowork): https://assets.claude.com/95a868946ac8a31e5ff832e2899f294aa368b836.png?w=32&h=32
- Cursor: https://cursor.com/marketing-static/favicon-light.svg, app tile removed and viewBox tightened around the symbol.
- Grok Bot: custom circular bot SVG based on the user's reference, black with white eyes. Not an official xAI asset.
- Grok symbol (alternate): paths from https://grok.com/images/favicon.svg, black without the app tile.

Served locally from `site/client-icons/`; selecting an app does not request images from these providers.
