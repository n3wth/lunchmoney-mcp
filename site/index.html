Your data. Clear boundaries.

Know what the connector can read and where that information goes.

Read-only by design

The service allows financial reads only. It cannot change your transactions or budgets. Your Lunch Money token may have broader permissions in Lunch Money.

Credentials stay out of chat

Nango stores your token. The connector retrieves it temporarily to make allowed requests to Lunch Money.

Your client receives the results

Financial results go to your AI client and may be sent to its model provider. That provider’s data settings apply.

Disconnect when you want

Run lunchmoney_disconnect to block further reads and delete the connection. Revoke the token itself in Lunch Money.
