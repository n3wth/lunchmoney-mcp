Connection guide

Remote MCP endpointhttps://mcp.lunchmoney.sh/mcp

  1. Sign in to the connector

    Auth0 handles sign-in at auth.n3wth.com, this project's custom login domain. It identifies your connector account; it does not sign you in to Lunch Money. The connector verifies your signed login and limits requests to your account's connection.

  2. Link your Lunch Money account

    Run lunchmoney_connect and open the returned browser link. Nango handles token entry and storage. Enter your Lunch Money token there, never in chat.

  3. Ask about your finances

    The connector reads the requested data from Lunch Money and returns it to your AI client. Your client and its model provider use those results to answer.

Direct setup is available. Marketplace listings are not yet published.

Your data. Clear boundaries.

Understand who handles your login, token, and financial results.

Reads enforced by the service

Cloudflare runs the connector. It retrieves your token from Nango temporarily and uses it for allowed reads from Lunch Money's API. The service cannot edit your finances, but the underlying token may have broader permissions.

A separate website and connection

Vercel hosts this website and routes MCP requests to Cloudflare. There is no token field on this page. Auth0 identifies your connector account; Nango stores the credential used to access Lunch Money.

Results leave the connector

The connector does not persist financial responses. Requested results go to your AI client and may be sent to its model provider. Their privacy and retention settings apply; hosting and authentication providers may also keep operational logs.

Disconnect and revoke separately

Run lunchmoney_disconnect to block further reads and request deletion of the Nango connection. Retry if deletion is pending. Revoke the token itself in Lunch Money. Uninstalling a plugin does neither.
