The service

The connector is a beta service that lets supported MCP clients request financial information from Lunch Money. The service enforces read-only access to financial data. Availability and compatibility may change, and the service may be changed or discontinued.

Your account and credentials

Connect only your own account or an account and data you are authorized to access. Use the service lawfully and do not try to bypass access controls, access another user's connection, or disrupt the service.

Keep your credentials private. Enter your Lunch Money token only in the browser connection flow provided by Nango, never in chat. The token may have broader permissions than this connector uses. You are responsible for reviewing requests from your AI client and protecting access to your account.

Financial results and AI answers

Results and AI-generated answers may be incomplete, delayed, or incorrect. Review them against your Lunch Money account before making decisions. The service does not guarantee accuracy or provide personalized financial advice.

Other providers and privacy

The service depends on Lunch Money, Auth0, Nango, Cloudflare, Vercel, and your selected AI client and model provider. Your use of those services is subject to their applicable terms and policies. Requested financial results are returned to your client and may be processed by its model provider.

The privacy notice explains how the connector handles credentials, account records, results, and operational logs.

Usage statistics

Basic usage counts are enabled by default. PostHog US Cloud receives landing-page view, Connect-click and successful prompt-copy events to help improve this website. No financial data, chat content, credentials, session recordings, or persistent visitor identifiers are included. We do not use analytics cookies. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals prevent collection. See the privacy notice for network processing and retention details.

You can disable these counts below. This preference is stored on this browser for 180 days and does not affect the service.

Stopping use

You may stop using the service at any time. Run lunchmoney_disconnect to block further reads and request deletion of the Nango connection; retry if deletion is pending. Revoke the token separately in Lunch Money. Uninstalling a plugin does not perform these steps. The privacy notice describes records retained after disconnecting.
